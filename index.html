FAQs

Questions? We have answers

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Give us a call and we’ll be happy to help.

Call +44 7887 306526
Do I need to provide cleaning products and equipment?

No. Our team brings everything needed. If you prefer us to use your own products, or want eco-friendly options, just let us know when booking.

Are you insured?

Yes. Our company is covered with public liability insurance and employer’s liability insurance, so you and your property are protected.

How is the price worked out?

Pricing depends on the size and condition of your property and the type of clean. We’ll give you a clear quote upfront, with no hidden charges.

Will an end of tenancy clean help me get my deposit back?

Our end of tenancy clean follows a detailed checklist to agency and landlord standards. If anything is missed, tell us and we’ll return to put it right.

Can you handle same-day Airbnb changeovers?

Yes. We work around your check-out and check-in times, including fresh linen and restocking, so your property is guest-ready on time.

Do I need to be at home during the clean?

Not at all. Many clients leave a key or access code with us. You are welcome to stay if you prefer.
